Parts list

# Part What it does Status
1 Cabinet Plywood or acrylic shell. Holds every part, with a paper-out slot, a coin-return hole and a lockable door. To build
2 Monitor Where the customer reads and clicks. Not touch — a mouse is used instead. Have
3 Mouse The only input device, since the monitor has no touch panel. Have
4 System unit Runs the Print Vendo app on Windows 10. All the software lives here. Have
5 Epson L121 printer Prints the pages. The app finds it by the name "Epson" or "L120 / L121". Have
6 Bond paper + Epson 664 ink Consumables. A4, Long and Short sizes are all supported by the app. To buy
7 1299 coin slot Accepts ₱1 / ₱5 / ₱10 / ₱20 and sends one pulse per peso. Needs its own 12V power. Ordered
8 Arduino UNO R3 Counts the pulses and sends the peso value over USB at 9600 baud. Signal wire goes to pin D2. Ordered
9 12V 2A adapter Powers the coin slot only — never the Arduino. The Arduino runs off the USB cable. Ordered
10 Jumper wires Male-to-female wires. The coin slot has bare wire ends; the Arduino has pins. Nothing connects without these. To buy
11 Locked coin box Catches and holds the money under the coin slot. A bowl works for testing. To buy
12 Surge protector Protects the system unit and printer from power spikes. Important on Philippine 220V lines. To buy
13 Phone hotspot (optional) Lets a customer scan the QR code and upload a file from their phone. Kiosk and phone must share one WiFi network. Free
The one rule that matters: the coin slot never plugs straight into the PC. It goes through the Arduino, which turns coin pulses into the peso numbers the app reads. Power the slot from the 12V adapter, share only the GND with the Arduino, and send the signal wire to pin D2.

Prices: ₱2 per page black & white, ₱4 per page color. Unused credit clears after 5 minutes idle.
